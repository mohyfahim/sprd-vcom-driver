# Code of conduct

Be respectful, technically honest, and constructive. Welcome people regardless
of background or experience, critique ideas rather than people, respect
privacy, and assume good faith while asking for reproducible evidence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct issues privately to `fahimohy@gmail.com`. The maintainer may
edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban participants when
necessary to keep the project safe and productive. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed.
